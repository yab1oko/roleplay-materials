## ✨ Overview
Roleplay Materials adds a small set of crafting and loot items for adventure and roleplay maps. Use it on its own as a materials pack for mapmakers, or let it feed crafts and drops in the rest of the YBLK lineup.

## 🧱 Features
Crafting and loot staples include scrap, wood, planks, cloth, pipes, nails, glue, tape, a sewing kit, and chemicals. Props and curiosities cover pebbles, old money, tickets, and keys, with multiple textures.

It optionally pairs with [Custom Loot System (CTS)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/custom-loot-system) and [Cold Weapons](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cold-weapons) for crafts and drops, and with [YBLK Fashion](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yblk-fashion) for gas filters, NVG batteries, and scrap repair plates. None of those mods require it to run.

## 🎮 How to use
Place the items as loot, crafting ingredients, or roleplay props in your map.
